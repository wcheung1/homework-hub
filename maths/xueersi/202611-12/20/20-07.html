There is a row of drinks in the vending machine, with a total of 18 cans. Counting from left to right, the cola can is the 12th can, and counting from right to left, the Sprite can is the 10th can. The Sprite can is to the left of the cola can. How many cans are there between the cola and the Sprite?

答：可樂罐和雪碧罐之間一共有 個罐子。

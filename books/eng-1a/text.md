Unit 1 A New School 
 Dear P.1 Parents and Students,  
  
Welcome to the start of P .1 English. At the beginning of each 
booklet, you will receive an outline of the important elements in the next few weeks’ English 
teaching.  
  
The first theme-based unit is ‘ A New School’. This unit helps the students learn all about 
the classroom, school and people who help us at school. In addition, there are activities to help 
everyone to get to know each other and establish classroo m routines. There is a checklist at the 
end of this booklet outlining the specific learning targets covered.  
  
Throughout their learning there will be opportunities for students to practise all four skills , 
namely Reading, Writing, Speaking and List ening. We will also include plenty of fun activities 
such as songs, games, rhymes, role play, etc.   
Reading   
Reading is an important and enjoyable element of learning in any language. 
To encourage 
students to develop good reading habits , the school has a number of initiatives, two of which 
are introduced below:   
 Epic!  
EPIC is an online reading platform . It consists of both fiction al and non-fictional readers 
from a variety of categories. Students are strongly recommended to explore it in their free time. 
Teachers will also share a list of theme -related recommended readers with students. 
 D.E.A.R. Calendar   
D.E.A.R is a daily reading scheme where children are encouraged to read every day. 
Please spend some time sharing a story with them to help build interest and develop a love of 
reading. The English teacher of your class will explain more about this to the students and set a 
monthly date for the ‘calendar’ to be returned to school to be checked .  
  
This year, students will also reflect on how they demonstrated certain character strengths 
on the reflection sheet at the end of each unit. This aligns with the school’s positive education 
focus.  
Do remember that we are here to help your children learn English in a positive and 
encouraging environment. Please feel free to contact us via email if you have any concerns about 
your child’s English learning.

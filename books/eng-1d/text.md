Unit 3 – Time to Play
Thursday 29th October 2026
Dear P1 Parents,

The school year continues to fly by and it's already the beginning of November! The children had great fun learning about 'My Family and I'. The next unit will certainly be one that is close to their hearts - 'Time to Play'.

Show-and-Tell
The highlight of this unit will be our show-and-tell time! Please allow your child to bring one favourite toy to school on Wednesday, 4th November. We will prepare a talk about it and return it at the end of the unit. The following are some of the questions that we would like to talk about:
● What is your toy?
● What colour is it?
● What size or shape is it?
● What can it do? (e.g. My robot can walk!)
● When do you play with it?
● Who do you share the toy with?
● Why do you like it so much?
The presentation will be conducted during the week of 9th November. We will also be looking for appropriate facial expressions, eye contact and spoken loud enough to be heard at all times.

Please make sure you label the toy with your child's name.

Phonics
Together with the phonemes from the previous 2 units, we will also include the phonemes v, w, y, z, j, n and e and letters q and x. The spelling and phonics quiz will be on Friday, 20th November.

Kind regards,
Ms. Mo (1A)　Mr. Horn (1B)　Mr. Ian　Ms. Annie (1D)

everybody getting the same thing.......

everybody getting what they need IN order to be SUCCESSFUL.
Discuss with your classmates and family.
*Why do you need to be fair when you play with others?
*What does it mean to be fair?
FAIRNESS
Play fair. Share. Treat everyone equally!
Fairness means...
Being honest and treating everyone the same in a caring and respectful way.
Take turns.
Everyone gets a chance to go.
Share and be kind.
Share what you have and include others.
Follow the rules.

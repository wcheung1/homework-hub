Unit 2 – ‘My Family and I’
7th October 2026

Dear P.1 Parents,

What a lovely start we have had by learning about our school. The children are beginning to settle into the English lesson routines. Both teachers and students are getting to know one another, and we are all enjoying it.

Our second theme-based unit is ‘My Family and I’. We will continue developing different skills in class and at home. In addition, your child will also be involved in activities such as songs, rhymes, games, show-and-tell, read aloud, role-play, etc. which contribute to raising their interest and enthusiasm in learning English. In this unit, students will be introduced to:
• the correct names of family members, for example: brother, sister, etc.
• names of different body parts
• words and phrases that can describe yourself, for example: My hair is black.
• naming words - nouns
• subject verb agreement
• subject pronouns such as ‘i’, ‘you’, ‘he’, ‘she’, ‘we’ and ‘they’
• sentences beginning with the phrases ‘This is……’ and ‘These are…’
• capitals and full stops.

We will hold a phonics and spelling quiz for each unit. We will start with the high frequency words and phonics quiz first. Gradually, we’ll be asking children to spell more theme-related words in their quizzes. Please use the ‘Look – Spell – Cover – Write – Check’ method to help your children learn the spelling words. (Please see page 5 of the booklet).

Reminder
The children will take their first theme-related reader home in this unit. Please take good care of the books and do not make any marks inside. To ensure all students get a chance to take home a reader, please return the readers along with the completed task on the due dates.

Kind regards,

Ms. Mo (1A)
Mr. Horn (1B)

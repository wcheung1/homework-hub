Two lists live on this page. ...
One list is sounds. One list is ten words. ...

Look at the sounds list. ...
R and rr. L and ll. D and dd. B and bb. F, ff and ph. ...
Here is a secret name for this. ...
Consonant digraph. ...
Two letters. One sound. ...
Two chopsticks pick up one piece of food. ...
Two sticks, one pick. ...
So rr is one r sound. Ll is one l sound. ...
Ff is one f sound. ...
And ph? Two letters. One f sound. Same as fish. ...
Phone starts with p and h. But it sounds like f. ...
Some children read p... h. That breaks the team. ...
Keep the team together. Say f. ...

Now the ten words. ...
These ten words come again and again. ...
In storybooks. In tests. In spelling time. ...
Learn them by heart. ...
Can. She. We. Me. My. ...
Have. Are. This. There. They. ...

In the test, write: Can. She. We. Me. My. Have. Are. This. There. They. ...

First trap. ...
This, there, they. ...
Three words start with th. ...
Many children mix them up. ...
Do not look at the start. All three look the same there. ...
Look at the end. ...
This ends with i... s. ...
There ends with e... r... e. ...
They ends with y. ...
The end tells you which one it is. ...

Next trap. Me or my. ...
One small letter makes the difference. ...
Me ends with e. ...
My ends with y. ...
Y at the end? That is my. ...

Last trap. Phone. ...
You already met this one. ...

Remember the chopsticks. ...

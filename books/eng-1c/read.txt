Guess. Can small sounds win a game? ...

Spin Read Write is a game for two. You need a paperclip and a pencil. Put the paperclip on the wheel. Flick it. It picks a word.

How do you play? One. Spin a word. Two. Sound out the phonemes. Phonemes are the small sounds in a word. Three. Read the word. Four. Write it in a square. Five. Four in a row wins. ...

Why sound out first? A big word is made of small sounds. Watch. Kuh... ah... tuh... Cat. Say them slow. Then say them fast.

Clap your hands for each sound. Kuh clap. Ah clap. Tuh clap. Cat.

Careful. Some kids say the letter name. Say the sound. Buh. Not bee.

Now the writing. Use your finger first. Write cat in the air. Big and slow. Then write it in a square. One sound, one letter. That helps your hand.

Use this trick at home. A new word on a box. Sound it out. You can read it by yourself. You can even draw your own wheel. Use family words. Mum. Dad. Me.

Now you try. I say buh... eh... duh. Guess the word. ... Say them fast. Join them. Buh. Eh. Duh. Bed. A wrong guess is okay. Joining sounds is the trick.

Remember this. Sound by sound, the word is found. Spin it. Sound it. Read it. Write it. Four in a row. Go!
